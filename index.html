You shouldn't have to guess which half of your budget is working.

AI in my workflow

AI does the first draft. I own the number.

I use AI every day, in my day job and across my own client accounts. It's wired into content, reporting, ad reviews, and store maintenance. Anything that goes out under my name gets read and approved by me first. Here's what runs where.

WorkflowWhat runs on AIWhat stays with me

Blog content

What runs on AI

Drafts each post from the client's brief and published log, then runs four passes: voice edit, adversarial edit, a second voice edit, and a fact-check. A post that can't be verified is held, not patched.

What stays with me

Topic choice and final approval. I pick from proposed options, and every post gets my read before it publishes.

Monthly client reporting

What runs on AI

Pulls results from Shopify, Analytics, Search Console, and Google Business Profile, then drafts each client's update in the channel that client actually reads, email or text.

What stays with me

Review and send. The numbers come from the platforms, not the model, and nothing sends on its own.

Paid media reviews

What runs on AI

Weekly Google Ads and Meta Ads reviews: tracking health, search terms, pacing, frequency, creative fatigue. It also flags ads still spending on sold-out products.

What stays with me

Every change. Reviews are read-only, and recommendations go into an approval queue.

Ecommerce operations

What runs on AI

Weekly product feed cleanup so Google Merchant Center and Meta stop disapproving listings. Friday email drafts built from live inventory and the store's latest video.

What stays with me

Judgment calls. Titles, junk listings, and anything ambiguous go to a review queue instead of auto-applying. Emails are drafted, never sent.

Tracking and measurement

What runs on AI

Audits GA4, Google Tag Manager, and Google Ads conversions for double counting, missing events, and import errors.

What stays with me

Sign-off on fixes. Changes are approval-gated and tested before I trust the data.

The rules it runs under.

01

Draft, then approve

Nothing sends or publishes under my name until I've read it.

02

Facts only

No invented numbers, no placeholders. If a claim can't be sourced, the work stops.

03

Client voice first

Each client gets a voice profile built from their real writing. Generic AI copy gets edited out.

04

Read before write

Anything that touches a live account starts read-only.
